## Research question

How should a baseline comparison of discrete eigenvalue solvers be recorded so that accuracy,
runtime, and reproducibility remain visible together?

## Mathematical formulation

For demonstration, consider the generalized matrix eigenproblem

```text
A u = lambda M u,
```

where `A` is symmetric positive definite and `M` is a mass matrix. The smallest eigenpairs are the
quantities of interest.

## Methods

- Dense reference solve for a small matrix.
- Sparse iterative solve with a fixed convergence tolerance.
- Residual verification using `||A u - lambda M u||_2`.

## Experimental setup

The example assumes one synthetic matrix at three resolutions. A real entry would identify the
matrix construction, software versions, solver options, and retained artifacts.

## Metrics

- Relative eigenvalue error against the reference solve.
- Eigenpair residual norm.
- Wall-clock time after warm-up.

## Results

## Interpretation

No scientific interpretation is claimed. A future real record should distinguish discretization
error, algebraic solver error, and timing variability.

## Limitations

- Synthetic values do not validate a method.
- A single matrix family cannot establish robustness.
- Timing methodology has not been defined for this demonstration.
