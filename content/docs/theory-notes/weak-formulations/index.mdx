## Model structure

For a scalar diffusion problem, multiply the strong equation by a test function `v`, integrate over
the domain, and transfer one derivative from the trial function to the test function. The resulting
abstract problem has the form

```text
Find u in V such that a(u, v) = l(v) for every v in V.
```

The bilinear form `a` records the differential operator and boundary conditions, while `l` records
forcing and natural boundary data.
