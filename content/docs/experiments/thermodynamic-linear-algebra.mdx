This interactive notebook demonstrates how the inverse of a symmetric
positive-definite matrix can be estimated from the time-averaged covariance of
overdamped Langevin dynamics in a quadratic potential.

The notebook is by [Simone Conradi](https://profconradi.com/) and is adapted
from the public [marimo gallery example](https://github.com/marimo-team/gallery-examples/blob/main/notebooks/math/thermodynamic_linear_algebra.py).

<Callout title="Interactive stochastic demonstration">
  Edit the symmetric matrix and adjust the integration, inverse-temperature,
  and damping controls. The trajectory and covariance estimate are computed
  in your browser. Individual estimates vary because the simulation includes
  thermal noise.
</Callout>
